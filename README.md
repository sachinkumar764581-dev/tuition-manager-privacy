# Privacy Policy for Tuition Manager (Tuition Manager Pro)
Effective Date: November 2026

## 1. Overview & Data Security
Tuition Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is an institute and student fee management application. We secure your educational and financial data with 256-bit SSL encryption, single-device session locking, 6-Digit Master PIN protection, and persistent Google Firebase cloud backups.

## 2. Information We Collect
To provide institute management features, the app stores:
- **Institute & Director Details:** Institute name, director name, email address, and phone number for account login and authentication.
- **Student & Fee Records:** Student names, batch details, admission dates, phone numbers, and fee payment records entered by the institute administrator.

## 3. Data Ownership & Zero Third-Party Sharing
You retain 100% absolute ownership of your data. We never sell, rent, track, or share your institute or student details with any third-party advertisers or trackers. You can export your complete fee ledger and student database anytime in Microsoft Excel (.xlsx) format.

## 4. Data Deletion & User Control
Institute administrators can edit or delete student records directly inside the app at any time. To request complete account and cloud data deletion, please contact us via our official support email below.
